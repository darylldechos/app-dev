## Plot Summary

A young girl named **Chihiro** wanders into a *spirit world* while moving
to a new home. To free her parents, she must work at a bathhouse run by
the witch ~~Yubaba~~ **Yubaba**, where she learns to be brave and independent.

##  Main Characters

1. **Chihiro** – the determined young heroine
2. **Haku** – a mysterious boy who helps her
3. **Yubaba** – the powerful bathhouse owner
4. **No-Face** – a silent, curious spirit
   - Appears first at the bathhouse entrance
   - Changes behavior depending on what he is offered

## Why I Love It

- Beautiful hand-drawn animation
- A memorable soundtrack
- Themes of:
  - Growing up
  - Greed and kindness
  - Identity and responsibility
